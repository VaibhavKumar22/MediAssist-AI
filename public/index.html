Common Symptoms

Headache

Pain in the head or upper neck, can be throbbing or constant. Common causes include stress, dehydration, or eye strain.

Fever

Body temperature above normal (98.6°F/37°C). Often indicates infection or illness. Monitor temperature regularly.

Cough

Sudden expulsion of air from the lungs. Can be dry or produce mucus. Common in colds and respiratory infections.

Fatigue

Extreme tiredness or lack of energy. Can be caused by physical or mental exertion, stress, or underlying conditions.

Nausea

Feeling of unease in the stomach with an urge to vomit. Can be caused by various factors including food poisoning or motion sickness.

Dizziness

Feeling of lightheadedness or unsteadiness. Can be caused by low blood pressure, dehydration, or inner ear problems.

Muscle Pain

Aches or discomfort in muscles. Can be caused by overuse, injury, or underlying conditions like fibromyalgia.

Shortness of Breath

Difficulty breathing or feeling of not getting enough air. Can be caused by anxiety, asthma, or heart conditions.
